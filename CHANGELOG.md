# Changelog

The module got its own repository at version 1.2.0; the entries before it are summaries.

## 1.3.0

- Tools in the shared controls can react to double-clicks on the map (`doubleClick`).

## 1.2.0

- Selection memory: the family's windows act on the tokens you selected last, even after you clicked into a window.
- The shared undo history (Ctrl+Z) guards against runaway depth, and uploads are checked before they are used.
